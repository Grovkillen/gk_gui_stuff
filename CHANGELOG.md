# Changelog

All notable changes to GK GUI Stuff are documented in this file.

## 1.04.000 (2026-11-08)

### Added

* The pill has a quiet form for a line of quiet text, such as an issue's history: `quiet: true` dims it in its own role like the text around it, in the light and the dark scheme alike, and makes it clear under the pointer and in focus; `struck: true` strikes its text through.
* A pill in the searchable chooser shows its type's icon first when the option names one (`data-gk-gui-icon`, with the type's name for the tooltip in `data-gk-gui-icon-label`).
* The searchable chooser for lists can be asked for on any list, whatever its length (`data-gk-gui-select="on"`), and can let what was typed be added as a new choice (`data-gk-gui-select="create"`): when the typed text is no choice exactly (case ignored, accents kept; `data-gk-gui-also` on an option gives more words that match), a row "Add: …" with the add icon stands last in the panel under a line, chosen with a click or with Enter when nothing else matches. The new choice is a pill with the part "new" in the role `data-gk-gui-select-new-role` gives; nothing is sent before the form is saved, and removing the pill removes the choice. `data-gk-gui-select-prefix` (such as #) is taken off what is typed and shown before the name, `data-gk-gui-select-lower` writes a new name in small letters, and with several choices a space, a comma or a semicolon finishes a name. For one who may not add (`data-gk-gui-select="create-denied"`) the last row says quietly that no tag has the name. A single choice with Add shows the chosen one as a pill whose cross empties the field and opens it again. GK Tags uses it for the tag field and a tag's parent.
* GkGui.fly, the fold-out box fastened to the button that opens it like a tab: a `<details class="gk-gui-stuff-fly">` with its box in `.gk-gui-stuff-fly-box`. It grows towards the side with room, counting the edges of what would cut it (a list table, Redmine's scrolling box around it, the content area) as well as the window, and when neither side has the room it stays on the side with more and is made no wider than that; it takes the focus to its first field, and Esc, Cancel and a click outside close it with what was written kept; without the script the details opens in place. Drawn with `gk_gui_stuff_fly` and `gk_gui_stuff_fly_buttons`.
* The design language's primitives, for every plugin to draw with instead of forms of their own, loaded on every page: the pill (`gk_gui_stuff_pill`, with a category or alert role, a count, a note, a remove cross that posts or is a plain link, chosen, the whole pill as one link with the count in it, a last part with an arrow as a link of its own to a page (`part_href:`, mirrored right to left, never on a pill that is edited or chosen), and a long name cut short with … while the count and the arrow stay; `GkGui.pill` draws the same pill from a script), the counter (`gk_gui_stuff_count`, a number or a text such as "1 of 2 on"), the foldable group with an edge in its role that remembers its state (`gk_gui_stuff_group`), the action card (`gk_gui_stuff_action_card`, also in the role alarm), the mark for what only administrators see and the lock with Step up (`gk_gui_stuff_admin_only`, `gk_gui_stuff_lock`), bars in a grid on one vertical line (`gk_gui_stuff_bars`), the category picker (`gk_gui_stuff_category_picker`) and a surface in the upper layer that moves nothing (`GkGui.layer`, `gk_gui_stuff_layer_button`).
* Quick save: a rail on the row's end side shows whether it saves at once, is changed, saved (fading back) or not saved, with a quiet word that takes no room (`gk_gui_stuff_rail`, `GkGui.save`). The forms that save at once: switch, segment, icon toggle, round check, edit in place, a row with its own Save, and a handle for order (also Alt and arrow). A rail may ask for a reason before saving. A GK form marks a changed field that is not saved yet, and each box of it with its own Save says beside the button how many fields are changed and not saved, then "Saved" for 3 seconds after the save; when the save fails, the written values, the marks and the text come back.
* The colour roles for categories 1 to 12, the alert levels, administrators, muted and the rail, declared until a theme gives them.
* A style guide for administrators showing every primitive in all its states, drawn by its own helper, with light and dark when GK Theme Engine gives dark mode; linked from Administration, Features. It shows the chooser with Add, a new choice, the quiet row and one choice with Add; the pill in two parts, in every role, without a count, not split, cut short in a narrow place and right to left; and a form with two boxes that each save on their own, one that fails and one beside a box that saves at once.
* The category picker is a button with the chosen swatch (and the category's name with `text: true`) that opens a fold-out box with the twelve categories in a grid of four by three, then None (`gk_gui_stuff_category_picker(name, selected, labelledby:, none:, text:)`). A click, Space or Enter on a choice closes the box, the arrow keys only move the choice, and the radios stay in the form, so it works without JavaScript. `.gk-gui-stuff-tabular-row` is a row of Redmine's tabular form that may hold a fold-out box, with the metrics of its paragraphs.
* The pill: `button: { type: 'submit', name:, value: }` makes its text a button of its form; `.gk-gui-stuff-pill-row` lays pills on a row.
* The pill's parts can be buttons: `button:` makes the text and the count one button, `part_buttons:` adds parts after it that are buttons, each with its own tooltip and attributes, for a script that opens a layer under the pill. `open: true` marks the part that opened the layer (the layer sets it on its own), and `dashed: true` draws a quiet dashed pill for what is possible but not chosen. A pill with buttons holds no link and no remove link.
* The switch has its mount: the sign and the switch in one rounded frame, with the state in words (On, Off) in the switch. `reason:` decides the mode: off draws the switch alone in the frame; optional has the pen, the switch changes at once and the pen opens a box with the reason first; required has the pen with a red star, the switch is dimmed, says that it needs a reason and opens the box, whose button makes the change and is named after it ("Switch on"). A place of GK Records' reasons can be given as the mode. `elevate: true` shows the lock in the administrators' dashed edge until stepped up, the box offering Step up, and nothing to anyone who is not an administrator. The box opens under the switch and is a sheet at the bottom of a narrow screen; Esc, Cancel and a click outside close it, what was written stays until the change is saved. The round check has the same mount, without a frame when no reason is asked.
* A button with a reason (`gk_gui_stuff_reason_button`): required is "Save…" with a fold-out box holding the reason; optional is the button in two parts, the word and a pen that opens the same box, the reason being sent only while the box is open; off is the button alone, except for what cannot be undone (`role: :alarm`), which always asks in a box. It can be its own form (`url:`) or belong to the form around it. A required reason is checked before the box's button sends and by the server, never by the browser's required field. Enter in the reason sends, Shift and Enter is a new line.
* The handle's list can wait for the form around it (`gk_gui_stuff_order(name, applies: :save)` or `:apply`): the items send their order with the form, an item out of its first place gets the changed rail, and a note under the list says that the order applies when one saves or applies. The text by the box's Save counts the list as one change. The row editor's rows do the same when moved.
* GkGui.layer: `parent:` puts the layer right after an element and fastens it there, so that the tab order goes on into it; `opener:` gets `aria-expanded` while the layer is open and the focus back after; `width: 'wide'` is a panel of at most 64em. A click outside closes it (not one in a layer opened after it, in Redmine's date picker or completion, or in the notices), a second click on the server's layer button closes it, and the focus goes to the first field that is seen. `gk_gui_stuff_layer_button` takes `width:`.
* The colour roles of the line's bands (incoming, active, settled, closed, archived), of the line (done, now, now-soft, next, next-soft, expected, stroke, track), of what is chosen (ui choice, with -soft) and of the logic words (and, or, not), declared until a theme gives them and listed among the colour roles. The pill, the fold-out box and GkGui.roleClass take the role choice.
* The style guide shows the pill's parts as buttons, open and dashed; a fold-out box without a role and in two roles; the alarm button against Redmine's button style; the switch and the round check in every mode of the mount; the buttons with a reason in every mode; the handle that waits for Save and for Apply; a wide layer and a layer under a pill.
* The fold-out box: `icon_only: true` makes the icon alone its button, with the label as tooltip and no "…", and `ellipsis: false` leaves the "…" off a text button; `GkGui.fly.open(anchor, content, {role, onClose})` opens one from a script at a link it drew outside a menu, in the link's place; an entry in a menu never opens a fold-out box, but opens a surface in the upper layer and ends with "…". `.gk-gui-stuff-fly-hint`, `.gk-gui-stuff-fly-field` and `table.gk-gui-stuff-fly-table` for its content.
* The ribbon takes a role (`GkGui.ribbon.show({…, role})`, admin when none is given) and is drawn in it.
* The top band can carry other plugins' parts (`GkGuiStuff::TopBand.register(key, plugin:, condition:, order:)`, drawn with `gk_gui_stuff_band_part`): a state that holds while one works and says who is registered, such as who is at a shared computer. A part stands in the role for choices, or the colour role for a muted surface with white text (`--gk-role-ui-muted-fill`, by default the muted role mixed 40 % towards black, set by a theme like any other role) when nothing is chosen, with an icon, its words, a quiet rest on a computer's width and outlined buttons in the band's text colour; on a phone the short words and the short buttons. The band is one line of one height: the instance's role first in its colour, with its name and the copy button on a computer's width and only the word on a phone, then the parts. A part is drawn also in production and when the instance band is off; the instance band's setting decides only the instance's part. The style guide shows the band with a part chosen and with nobody chosen.
* The ribbon lets the pointer through: clicks and hovering reach what lies under it, and it grows more transparent while the pointer is over it (with no fading when reduced motion is asked for). A ribbon can have an action (`action: {label, href}` or `action: {label, handler}`): a small round button with Redmine's cancel icon, kept upright, the only part that takes the pointer, with the label as its tooltip and for screen readers; over the button the ribbon is fully seen. A locked page's button leads to the live page. `href:` on the ribbon itself is gone. The style guide shows a locked ribbon with its button, and one with an icon and its whole sentence.
* The ribbon runs from edge to edge across the corner, cut by both edges, with its word in the middle of what is seen. Its distance from the corner follows the length of the word, so that a long word and the button always fit; the button stands far down the band, a small gap from the edge, never over the word, and on a phone never over the middle of Redmine's menu button. While Redmine's menu is open on a phone the ribbon is hidden, so that it covers no row of the menu. A ribbon can have an icon before its word (`icon:`, drawn only when GK Icons is installed; otherwise the word stands alone) and a whole sentence (`title:`) that screen readers read in place of the word and that the button's tooltip shows before what the button does.
* The style guide shows the note as an icon, someone else's shared pill, a pill as a button, a fold-out box with only an icon and one from a script, the category picker with text, in a row and with none chosen, the row menu with Edit kept in the row, and the ribbon with a fold-out box in the real top menu.
* The line (`gk_gui_stuff_line(data, form:, mode:, remember:, legend:, vertical:)`, `GkGui.line`): a flow drawn from data in two halves, the work and the closing, with a stroke between them, a strip of bands on top, parallel lanes, the way gone and "expected now", and steps that have been, are now, can be chosen or are not allowed (a tick and an arrow in the dots, and a legend). Long labels are cut short with …; the second of two neighbours has its label lifted above the row while its dot stays on the line, and a label that still does not fit is cut to the room left, whole in its tooltip; below 899 px the line is vertical, with a heading per half (per lane in a half with more than one lane), the dots on a vertical line in the band's colour and the stroke as the row "The issue is closed here". The compact form shows a bar of bands, a line of text, the next steps as buttons (with a reason or the lock as the buttons with a reason do) and a fold button "Show the whole flow" that opens the detailed form as a wide panel in the layer; the open panel can be remembered per person. A step can save at once (`mode: :save`, the line is a rail), be chosen in a form and written into a select (`mode: :choose`), or be dragged along the line, across the stroke and between lanes, writing the inputs of a form (`form: :editable`), also with the arrow keys.
* The system's suggestion: `gk_gui_stuff_pill(..., suggested: true)` draws the pill dashed in the info role with the part "Suggestion", and `gk_gui_stuff_suggestion(text)` a row of its own.
* `gk_gui_stuff_group_toggle(closed_text, open_text)` draws the group's fold button, whose words can turn with its state (`gk_gui_stuff_group(..., texts: [closed, open])`); every click says so with the event `gk-gui-group-toggle`, and `GkGui.group.set(button, open)` sets it from a script. `GkGui.layer.open` takes `focus: false`, the lock takes `summary:` and `summary_class:`, and the button with a reason takes `submit_label:` for its box's button.
* The roles of the bands (`band-incoming` to `band-archived`) and the line's `line-next` and `line-now` are roles of the primitives (`role_class`, `GkGui.roleClass`).
* The style guide shows the line in all its forms (detailed, compact, the panel open, after a step that saves and one that fails, in a form, editable with a form of its own, vertical), the bands and the line's roles, the system's suggestion and the fold button with text.
* The style guide shows every primitive that exists, each with its call beside it: the chooser on any list, with several choices and as a fixed list with quiet pills, and the filter over a long list of check boxes; the words of the buttons; the box of choices above a list beside the row menu; the whisper, a short text in the tooltip and a card in Redmine's tooltip; the banner and the notices; the surface, the drawer, the wave, the corner, the palette and the opener; and an empty value edited in place. A link Try opens what opens, and the page says when no module that loads those elements is on. Every text and example on the page comes from the locale files.
* The sentence, `gk_gui_stuff_sentence` and `GkGui.sentence`: a lead, conditions as pills whose field, operator and value and cross are buttons, the words and, or and not in their logic roles, groups in quiet parentheses, quiet text, choices as whole pills, and a plain "+ Condition" button; with a frame or inline, its frame changed while something waits for Apply. `GkGui.sentence.box` opens a part's box under the pill with Done and "Remove the condition": Enter and a click outside are Done, Esc and the cross close without changes. `GkGui.sentence.note` and `gk_gui_stuff_sentence_note` write "Not applied: …" beside Apply.
* The searchable chooser is always loaded (`gk_gui_stuff_select.js`, `gk_gui_stuff_select.css`) and can be opened on any list with `GkGui.selectSearch.enhance(select, { open: true })`, which returns `open`, `close` and `destroy`; the module Searchable chooser decides only which lists get it by themselves. Option groups are headings in the muted role, and an option with `data-gk-gui-used="1"` is quiet with "(already in use)" after it.
* Meaning view and Classic view: a register of page types (`GkGuiStuff::Meaning.register`), filled by the plugins that own the pages; the segment Meaning view | Classic view first among a page's actions where each person may choose (`gk_gui_stuff_meaning_switch`), which saves and loads the page again with the same address; `GkGui.meaning.view(key)` for views drawn by a script. The administrator's page Administration > Meaning view and Classic view sets per page type Meaning view, Classic view or Each person chooses, with the default for those who choose, and a row that sets all pages; it is shown when a plugin has registered a page type. My account gets the row "Reset my choices" once a view has been chosen. Like Redmine for me gives the Classic view everywhere.
* The table of contents, `gk_gui_stuff_toc` and `GkGui.toc`: a row that follows at the top of a long page (below Redmine's fixed header on a phone) and marks the section being read.
* The colour roles as swatches with their names and custom properties, `gk_gui_stuff_role_swatch` and `gk_gui_stuff_role_swatches`, and the constant `GkGuiStuff::Primitives::ROLES`.
* `GkGui.pill` builds a pill whose text and parts are buttons, open or dashed, as the server's helper does. `GkGui.layer.open`'s `onClose` is told why the layer closed (escape, x, outside or api); `outside: 'click'` closes a layer on the click outside instead of the press, so that what the click lands on still gets it; a layer whose box another script removed is still cleaned up when it closes.
* The style guide is drawn from a register of sections (`GkGuiStuff::Styleguide`) in two parts, Redmine and GK, with the table of contents at the top.
* The part Redmine shows Redmine's own elements drawn by Redmine's own templates with records made up in memory and never saved: a labelled form with an error, buttons, the issue list grouped with totals (with Redmine's context menu by Try), the history, tabs, messages, pagination, the toolbar, tips, badges, progress, avatars and the watchers dialog shown still. New GK sections show the sentence, the switch and the chooser's groups. A section that cannot be drawn shows a warning and the rest of the page is drawn.
* The icon button (`gk_gui_stuff_icon_button(icon, label, href:, html:)`, `GkGui.iconButton({icon, label, fallback, href, onClick, className})`): Redmine's icon in a round hit area, in the colour of the text around it, with the label as its tooltip and for screen readers; a link with `href:`. It is for actions that have an accepted icon (close, reload, maximize, cancel) in what carries a message or a surface. `GkGui.banner.show`'s `button` and `GkGui.notice.show`'s `buttons` take `icon:` to draw one. The style guide has a section for it, with a banner and a notice to try.
* `GkGui.count({n, shared, role, title})` draws the count from a script as `gk_gui_stuff_count` does, and both take `shared:` to show own and shared apart ("2+3").
* The style guide is the function Style guide in GK Records' register, on by default: off, the page answers 404 and Administration > Functions has no link to it. Without GK Records, or with a GK Records whose register does not yet know a function on by default, the style guide is off.
* The pill takes the type's icon first (`type_icon:`, `type_label:`, and `typeIcon`, `typeLabel` in `GkGui.pill`): only to read, inside the pill's link or button, with no divider before the name, with "Type: …" as its tooltip from the helper and from the script alike; nothing for a tag without a type, nor where an icon pack's icon cannot be drawn (without GK Icons, or with the pack gone).
* A mark's note in its pill (`note_icon: true`) is a button: its tooltip is the note, and a click or a tap opens a small fold-out box with the heading Note (`note_label:`), quiet words after it (`note_hint:`), the whole note and, when `note_edit:` gives the address that changes it, Change the note, which opens the note's field with Save and Cancel in the same box; Save or Enter sends the note as PATCH, an error from the server shows in the box and a save loads the page again. Esc or a click outside closes it. `note_open: true` writes the note out after the icon, cut short with …, the icon and the text one part that opens the same box.
* `choice: true` (or `choice` in `GkGui.pill`) on a pill whose one button is its text makes it a choice in a chooser: its text is underlined on hover, a ring in its role stands 2 px outside it on keyboard focus, and `pressed: true` draws it chosen, filled in its role with the check first; `pressed:` sets `aria-pressed`. Other pills with buttons, such as the sentence's choices, keep their look.
* The alert levels tip, notice and critical, with the roles `--gk-role-alert-tip`, `-notice` and `-critical`, each with a soft tone: six levels from tip to critical, for pills, notices and banners. Critical is the alarm filled, with white text. `GkGui.notice` and `GkGui.banner` take the level tip.
* The neutral roles of the interface, with today's values as defaults: `--gk-role-ui-surface` (a box's surface), `-ui-frame` (the frame around boxes, fold-out boxes and fields), `-ui-divider` (the line between rows), `-ui-shadow` (the shadow's base colour, which each shadow mixes to its own strength), `-ui-code` with `-soft` (code text as a chip) and `-ui-possible` (the dashed pill). The fold-out box, the mount, the layer, the line, the chooser, the table of contents, the segment and the shadows of the shared elements use them, and the frames of the notice, the corner card, the link preview, the module menu and the row menu take `-ui-frame`. For Redmine's dark menus there are `--gk-role-ui-admin-on-dark` (the administrators' role mixed 40 % with its soft tone) and `--gk-role-alert-alarm-on-dark` (the alarm mixed 60 % with its soft tone), declared and shown as swatches like the others.
* `gk_gui_stuff_icon_button(:minimize, label)`: the icon for smaller, from GK Icons' own sprite; without GK Icons it is maximize with the label as its tooltip.
* `.gk-gui-stuff-choice-col` on a table's heading and cells centres a column of radio buttons and its heading over them.
* The style guide shows the type's icon in a pill, the note as a button and written out, a pill to choose at rest and chosen, the six alert levels as pills and as notices, the new roles as swatches (the shadow as a surface that casts it, the custom property as a chip of code text), the icon for smaller, a column of radio buttons with its heading, and a row menu entry that asks in a surface of the upper layer.
* The segment and the icon toggle take `reason:`, `elevate:`, `purpose:` and `state:` as the switch does and stand in the same mount: optional changes at once with the pen beside it, required dims the control and opens the box, whose button names the change ("Change to <choice>" and "Changes <label> to <choice>." for a segment, "Switch on <label>" and "Switch off <label>" for an icon toggle); with no reason they stand bare as before.
* Edit in place takes `reason:`, `elevate:` and `purpose:`: off is Save and Cancel, optional is [ Save | pen ] and Cancel, the pen opening the reason box under the field, required is "Save…" and Cancel, Save… opening the box whose Save saves with the reason; `elevate: true` shows the lock until stepped up.
* Below 899 px every fold-out box, the box of a mount, of a rail's reason and of edit in place, and every surface of the upper layer opens as a sheet at the bottom of the window, with a grip on top, the same content and Cancel (added where the box has none); the page behind is dimmed, does not scroll, and a press on it closes the sheet; the focus stays in the sheet, and a fold-out box without a name of its own is read with its button's name. `GkGui.sheet` gives the same to a script's own box.
* The line marks steps on the same half, place and lane in every form: their dots get an edge in the alarm role and a row under the line says "2 steps stand on the same place: A and B." with Show, which takes the focus to the steps (the compact form opens its panel first); in the editable form it follows the drag, and the steps' table rows are marked in the alarm role.
* Administration > Meaning view and Classic view asks for a reason at the place `gui_stuff.meaning` in GK Records (optional by default): a row's segments stand in their mount, the row for all pages has three buttons with a reason, and every change is an event in GK Records with its reason; each row shows its latest change quietly in its name cell, with History.
* `.gk-gui-stuff-menu-admin` on a row in Redmine's account menu: a row only administrators see. Where the account is links in the top menu, it is a link after My account, underlined in the administrators' role for a dark menu (`--gk-role-ui-admin-on-dark`); in Redmine's menu on a phone it has an edge in that role; where the account is a dropdown it is not shown there on a computer's width. `.gk-gui-stuff-menu-off` dims the row of one who cannot use it, with the reason in a `small` on its own line, and `.gk-gui-stuff-menu-alarm` gives the reserve account's row an edge in the alarm role for a dark menu (`--gk-role-alert-alarm-on-dark`).
* `gk_gui_stuff_sg_row(state, call, wide: true)` gives the style guide's drawing the table's whole width; every row of the line uses it. The style guide shows the segment, the icon toggle and edit in place with their reasons, a segment that fails, a fold-out box without Cancel of its own for the sheet, and the line with crowded labels and a collision.
* Every module, and the settings that are not modules, are appearance settings in GK Records' register: each with its group on the page Appearance, its levels, its values and GK's proposal. Settings of their own: the account menu's link colours and the boxes at the start or the end (both rules that were always on before), the counter at the name (shown as what waits, split by kind or with zero), the buttons' placement, the project menu and the buttons as icons, the sidebar's place (before or after the content, or hidden, its buttons saying Left and Right as they stand on the page, so that they swap in a right-to-left language) and the header's height. The fold's height, the notices' time, due soon and the heartbeat are shared values that several settings read; a value with more to say has a quiet help text under it, as Redmine's forms have (that warnings stay until closed, that 0 days also turns off the state Issues due soon in GK Theme Engine's palette, what the heartbeat costs).
* A type of Redmine's own pages in the Meaning register (`core: true`) is an appearance setting: off is the Classic view, which is how a new installation starts; optional lets each person choose, with a default; forced is the Meaning view.
* The reason place Appearance, change, for the form that sets the appearance settings.
* Scenario step `gui_stuff`: `appearance: { <setting key>: off | optional | forced }` sets any plugin's appearance setting; `modules:` sets gui_stuff's own by the module's name. Scenario checks `appearance_mode` and `appearance_on_for`.
* Administration, Appearance: every GK plugin's appearance settings in one form, beside Functions in the administration menu and in the same group. The settings stand in groups by where they show (The instance and the menus, Lists, The issue page, Forms and settings, Text and links, Calendar and Gantt, Messages and notices, Administration), with the shared values last. The page is one table: the column headings once, and each group a part of its own under its fold button with the count of its settings that are on. Each setting's level is a radio button under Off, Optional and Forced; a setting that applies to all or none says "applies to all" under Optional; a setting that the running Redmine has built in says "Built into Redmine <version>" over the three columns, with a quiet help text under it that GK adds nothing, ending in the path to Redmine's own setting where there is one; without such a setting the row is dimmed, has no radio buttons and cannot be chosen; a setting whose plugin is missing is dimmed with its buttons inactive and names the plugin it needs. A setting's values stand under it while it is not off, and a value that is a table or a list of ways (the buttons' placement, the administration's rows, the status of the quick done button, what is hidden in Redmine's pages, where Help leads) is folded with a summary. The shared values have no level: each has its description and its fields under it, always shown, with the settings that use it. Each row shows its plugin, Show, what it goes with, and its latest change with the reason in quotes and a link to its history; a change of values only says "Values changed". Below 899 px the form never scrolls sideways: each row stacks, with the name and its description on top and the levels under them as buttons in full width with their words (Off, Optional, Forced), "applies to all" in the place of Optional where it cannot be chosen, and the rail of a changed row at the end, as on a computer; a table among a row's values stacks one cell under the other.
* Like Redmine (every setting off) and GK's proposal stand as links with an icon, drawn as Redmine draws its own links at the top of a page: on a computer's width in the top row before the line that the changes apply when saved, and on a narrow screen at the top right beside the heading, on their own line above it, as Redmine's own links do; they only fill in the form and save nothing, which their tip and the name a screen reader reads after the label say. The top row says that the changes apply when saved, and has a search over names, descriptions and plugins (Enter in it never sends the form); on a computer's width the search stands first and the line at the end of the row. The bar at the bottom of the form is always in sight with the count of changed rows, Undo all and Save. Save takes one reason for every change by the reason place Appearance, change; its box lists the changes, and on a phone it opens as a sheet at the bottom.
